Our Story

Find your wildflower moment.

Wildflower Bliss Lodge began with a simple dream: to create a peaceful mountain retreat where guests could slow down, reconnect and experience the natural beauty of Colorado.

Nestled among pine trees and colorful mountain wildflowers, our two-story timber lodge offers the warmth of a traditional Colorado cabin and a porch that invites you to stay just a little longer.

A clear mountain creek winds through the property, creating quiet places to read, enjoy your morning coffee or simply listen to the water beneath the Colorado sky.

Whether you're joining us for a romantic weekend, a mountain adventure or a peaceful escape, we hope Wildflower Bliss Lodge is where the loveliest part of the day might be the hour you leave unplanned.
